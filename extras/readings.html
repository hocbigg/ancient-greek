Great Readings in Ancient Greek

This curated collection gathers field-defining monographs, landmark essays, and influential intellectual studies in Ancient Greek philology, literature, and culture. These works explore the mechanics of oral composition, reconstruct archaic and classical mentalities, analyze the civic performance of tragedy, and trace how ancient texts survived antiquity.
