# Great Courses

This curated collection highlights high-impact university lecture series, full-length OpenCourseWare offerings, and specialized open educational masterclasses in Ancient Greek language, literature, philosophy, and classical history.

## Greek Language, Historical Linguistics, and Philology

- [Learn Ancient Greek (YouTube / Harvard Center for Hellenic Studies & Brandeis University / Leonard Muellner & Belisi Gillespie)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLq5ea-jR9u2puDaLoRL-nBkpwrkURbLjT)
- [Introduction to Comparative Indo-European Linguistics (Coursera / Leiden University / Alwin Kloekhorst)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/comparative-indo-european-linguistics)
- [Sunoikisis Digital Classics (Sunoikisis DC / University of Leipzig & Institute of Classical Studies / Monica Berti & Gabriel Bodard)](https://sunoikisisdc.github.io/)
