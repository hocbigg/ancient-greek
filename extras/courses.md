# Great Courses

This curated collection highlights high-impact university lecture series, full-length OpenCourseWare offerings, and specialized open educational masterclasses in Ancient Greek language, literature, philosophy, and classical history.

## Foundational & Intensive Language Acquisition

- [Learn Ancient Greek (Center for Hellenic Studies / Harvard University & Brandeis / Leonard Muellner & Belisi Gillespie)](https://chs.harvard.edu/learning-ancient-greek/)
- [Ancient Greek Tutorials (UC Berkeley / Donald J. Mastronarde)](https://atticgreek.org/)
- [Discovering Ancient Greek and Latin (OpenLearn / The Open University)](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/discovering-ancient-greek-and-latin/content-section-0)

## Epic Poetics, Myth, and Dramatic Performance

- [The Ancient Greek Hero (edX / Harvard University / Gregory Nagy)](https://www.edx.org/learn/humanities/harvard-university-the-ancient-greek-hero)
- [Greek and Roman Mythology (Coursera / University of Pennsylvania / Peter Struck)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/mythology)
- [Reading Greek Tragedy Online (Center for Hellenic Studies & Out of Chaos Theatre / Joel Christensen & Paul O'Mahony)](https://chs.harvard.edu/programs/reading-greek-tragedy-online/)

## Ancient Philosophy and Political Thought

- [Ancient Philosophy: Plato and His Predecessors (Coursera / University of Pennsylvania / Susan Sauvé Meyer)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/plato)
- [Ancient Philosophy: Aristotle and His Successors (Coursera / University of Pennsylvania / Susan Sauvé Meyer)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/aristotle)
- [Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Yale University / Steven B. Smith)](https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-114)
- [Classical Greek Philosophy (History of Philosophy Without Any Gaps / LMU Munich & King's College London / Peter Adamson)](https://historyofphilosophy.net/series/classical-greek-philosophy)

## Classical Greek History, Historiography, and Material Culture

- [Introduction to Ancient Greek History (Open Yale Courses / Yale University / Donald Kagan)](https://oyc.yale.edu/classics/clcv-205)
- [The Ancient Greeks (Coursera / Wesleyan University / Andrew Szegedy-Maszak)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ancient-greeks)
- [Faculty of Classics Public Lectures and Seminars (University of Oxford Podcasts / Faculty of Classics)](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/units/faculty-classics)
