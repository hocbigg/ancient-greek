Resources

Vocabulary

  • Dickinson College Commentaries Core Ancient Greek Vocabulary - List of the 500 and 1,000 most frequent words in Ancient Greek, complete with definitions, frequency rankings, audio, and downloadable spreadsheets.
  • Logeion (University of Chicago) - Fast, modern multi-lexicon search engine providing simultaneous results from LSJ, Middle Liddell, Autenrieth, Bailly, and frequency statistics.
  • Perseus Word Study Tool - Morphological analysis engine that identifies lemmas, parses inflected cases and conjugations, and links directly to dictionary definitions.
  • Woodhouse's English-Greek Dictionary (University of Chicago) - Searchable online edition of S.C. Woodhouse's standard reverse lexicon for prose composition and translation.
  • AnkiWeb Shared Decks for Ancient Greek - Community-curated spaced-repetition flashcard decks covering vocabulary from standard textbooks (Athenaze, Hansen & Quinn), core frequency lists, and principal parts.

Grammar

  • Herbert Weir Smyth's Greek Grammar - The standard comprehensive reference grammar for classical Attic Greek, fully digitized and cross-linked.
  • Ancient Greek Tutorials (Donald J. Mastronarde / UC Berkeley) - Structured interactive web tutorials featuring native audio pronunciation, accent rules, drill exercises, and full inflection paradigms.
  • Learn Ancient Greek Playlist (Leonard Muellner / Harvard CHS) - Complete 114-lecture open university course covering two full semesters of college-level classical Attic grammar.
  • Textkit Greek and Latin Learning Tools - Large library of free public-domain Greek grammars, primers, answer keys, and readers available as downloadable PDFs.
  • Ancient Greek for Everyone - Free, open-access introductory digital textbook emphasizing core patterns, vocabulary building, and syntax fundamentals.
  • DCC Greek Grammar Guide - Searchable web edition of Goodell's School Grammar of Attic Greek, cross-linked directly with Dickinson text commentaries.

Reading & Comprehensible Input

  • Geoffrey Steadman's Greek Commentaries - Free student editions of unadapted texts (Plato, Xenophon, Lysias, Homer, Euripides) with running facing-page vocabularies and grammatical notes.
  • Perseus Digital Library - Vast open digital corpus of classical Greek literature equipped with hyperlinked translations, commentaries, and morphological analysis for every word.
  • Dickinson College Commentaries - Web-based annotated editions of Greek prose and verse featuring inline grammatical explanations, vocabulary lists, and historical commentary.
  • First1KGreek (Open Greek and Latin Project) - Comprehensive open-access digital initiative providing machine-readable, canonical Greek texts from Homer through late antiquity.
  • Bibliotheca Augustana (Graeca) - Well-curated chronological digital library of full-text classical, Hellenistic, and Byzantine Greek literature.

Writing & Composition

  • Athenaze Greek Exercises (University of Victoria) - Interactive online exercises aligned with the Athenaze textbook series, including morphological drills, Cloze tests, and translation composition.
  • North & Hillard Greek Prose Composition (Textkit) - Classic systematic textbook for learning to compose idiomatic Attic Greek sentences, accompanied by a downloadable answer key.
  • Pandionis Interactive Audio Exercises - Free interactive exercises focused on active oral and written sentence construction using historical reconstructed pronunciation.
  • Sidgwick's Introduction to Greek Prose Composition (Internet Archive) - Advanced manual covering Greek sentence architecture, idiom, particles, and continuous narrative composition with extensive notes.

Listening & Spoken Greek

  • Polis Institute Audio Resources - High-quality spoken recordings of classical and Koine Greek texts produced using active living-language immersion methods.
  • ScorpioMartianus (Luke Ranieri / YouTube) - Immersion videos, spoken dialogues, and pedagogical guides illustrating reconstructed historical Attic and Koine pronunciation.
  • Ioannis Stratakis / Podium Arts (YouTube) - Expressive readings of Homer, Plato, and lyric poetry focusing on accurate metrical rhythm and classical pitch accent.
  • Alpha with Angela (YouTube) - Comprehensible input video lessons teaching spoken Koine and Ancient Greek naturally through physical actions, storytelling, and visual context.
  • Rhapsodoi Ancient Greek Recordings (SoundCloud) - Curated collection of spoken excerpts from major ancient authors performed with reconstructed pronunciation.
  • SORGLL (Society for the Oral Reading of Greek and Latin Literature) - Archival audio repository demonstrating authentic recitation techniques for epic hexameter, tragic meters, and rhetorical prose.

Cultural & Historical Context

  • Introduction to Ancient Greek History (Open Yale Courses) - Full 24-lecture university video series by Donald Kagan tracing ancient Greek civilization from the Bronze Age through the fall of Athens.
  • Ancient Greece Declassified Podcast - Accessible, interview-based scholarly podcast exploring ancient Greek philosophy, political theory, archaeology, and literature.
  • Center for Hellenic Studies (Harvard University) - Academic hub providing open-access monographs, scholarly articles, lecture series, and digital publications on Hellenic literature and society.

Digital Tools & Study Aids

  • Alpheios Reading Tools - Free browser extension that provides instant morphological parsing, inflection tables, and dictionary definitions when double-clicking Greek words on any website.
  • Diogenes - Free desktop software designed for fast, granular lexical searches across classical databases (TLG, PHI) with integrated LSJ lexica.
  • Greek Font Society (GFS) - Archive of free, open-source digital typefaces based on historical classical and Renaissance Greek typography.
  • Learning Ancient Greek (The Open University / OpenLearn) - Introductory study guides, taster modules, and independent learning advice for self-directed classicists.
