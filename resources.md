# Resources

## Vocabulary

- [Dickinson College Commentaries Core Ancient Greek Vocabulary](https://dcc.dickinson.edu/greek-core-list) - List of the 500 and 1,000 most frequent words in Ancient Greek, complete with definitions, frequency rankings, audio, and downloadable spreadsheets.
- [Logeion (University of Chicago)](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/) - Fast, modern multi-lexicon search engine providing simultaneous results from LSJ, Middle Liddell, Autenrieth, Bailly, and frequency statistics.
- [Perseus Word Study Tool](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/morph) - Morphological analysis engine that identifies lemmas, parses inflected cases and conjugations, and links directly to dictionary definitions.
- [Woodhouse's English-Greek Dictionary (University of Chicago)](https://artflsrv03.uchicago.edu/efts/woodhouse/) - Searchable online edition of S.C. Woodhouse's standard reverse lexicon for prose composition and translation.
- [AnkiWeb Shared Decks for Ancient Greek](https://ankiweb.net/shared/decks/ancient%20greek) - Community-curated spaced-repetition flashcard decks covering vocabulary from standard textbooks (*Athenaze*, *Hansen & Quinn*), core frequency lists, and principal parts.

## Grammar

- [Herbert Weir Smyth's Greek Grammar](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.04.0007) - The standard comprehensive reference grammar for classical Attic Greek, fully digitized and cross-linked.
- [Ancient Greek Tutorials (Donald J. Mastronarde / UC Berkeley)](https://atticgreek.org/) - Structured interactive web tutorials featuring native audio pronunciation, accent rules, drill exercises, and full inflection paradigms.
- [Learn Ancient Greek Playlist (Leonard Muellner / Harvard CHS)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLq5ea-jR9u2puDaLoRL-nBkpwrkURbLjT) - Complete 114-lecture open university course covering two full semesters of college-level classical Attic grammar.
- [Textkit Greek and Latin Learning Tools](https://www.textkit.com/learn-greek/) - Large library of free public-domain Greek grammars, primers, answer keys, and readers available as downloadable PDFs.
- [Ancient Greek for Everyone](https://ancientgreek.pressbooks.com) - Free, open-access introductory digital textbook emphasizing core patterns, vocabulary building, and syntax fundamentals.
- [DCC Greek Grammar Guide](https://dcc.dickinson.edu/grammar/greek/) - Searchable web edition of Goodell's *School Grammar of Attic Greek*, cross-linked directly with Dickinson text commentaries.

## Reading & Comprehensible Input

- [Geoffrey Steadman's Greek Commentaries](https://geoffreysteadman.com/) - Free student editions of unadapted texts (Plato, Xenophon, Lysias, Homer, Euripides) with running facing-page vocabularies and grammatical notes.
- [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/) - Vast open digital corpus of classical Greek literature equipped with hyperlinked translations, commentaries, and morphological analysis for every word.
- [Dickinson College Commentaries](https://dcc.dickinson.edu) - Web-based annotated editions of Greek prose and verse featuring inline grammatical explanations, vocabulary lists, and historical commentary.
- [First1KGreek (Open Greek and Latin Project)](https://opengreekandlatin.github.io/First1KGreek/) - Comprehensive open-access digital initiative providing machine-readable, canonical Greek texts from Homer through late antiquity.
- [Bibliotheca Augustana (Graeca)](https://www.hs-augsburg.de/~harsch/augustana.html) - Well-curated chronological digital library of full-text classical, Hellenistic, and Byzantine Greek literature.

## Writing & Composition

- [Athenaze Greek Exercises (University of Victoria)](https://web.uvic.ca/hrd/greek/) - Interactive online exercises aligned with the *Athenaze* textbook series, including morphological drills, Cloze tests, and translation composition.
- [North & Hillard Greek Prose Composition (Textkit)](https://www.textkit.com/greek_grammar_prose_composition.php) - Classic systematic textbook for learning to compose idiomatic Attic Greek sentences, accompanied by a downloadable answer key.
- [Pandionis Interactive Audio Exercises](https://pandionis.com/intro_en.html) - Free interactive exercises focused on active oral and written sentence construction using historical reconstructed pronunciation.
- [Sidgwick's Introduction to Greek Prose Composition (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/introductiontogr00sidgrich) - Advanced manual covering Greek sentence architecture, idiom, particles, and continuous narrative composition with extensive notes.

## Listening & Spoken Greek

- [Polis Institute Audio Resources](https://www.polisjerusalem.org/resources/audios/) - High-quality spoken recordings of classical and Koine Greek texts produced using active living-language immersion methods.
- [ScorpioMartianus (Luke Ranieri / YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ScorpioMartianus) - Immersion videos, spoken dialogues, and pedagogical guides illustrating reconstructed historical Attic and Koine pronunciation.
- [Ioannis Stratakis / Podium Arts (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@podiumarts) - Expressive readings of Homer, Plato, and lyric poetry focusing on accurate metrical rhythm and classical pitch accent.
- [Alpha with Angela (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@alphawithangela) - Comprehensible input video lessons teaching spoken Koine and Ancient Greek naturally through physical actions, storytelling, and visual context.
- [Rhapsodoi Ancient Greek Recordings (SoundCloud)](https://soundcloud.com/rhapsodoi/sets/ancient-greek-recordings) - Curated collection of spoken excerpts from major ancient authors performed with reconstructed pronunciation.
- [SORGLL (Society for the Oral Reading of Greek and Latin Literature)](http://www.sorgll.org/) - Archival audio repository demonstrating authentic recitation techniques for epic hexameter, tragic meters, and rhetorical prose.

## Cultural & Historical Context

- [Introduction to Ancient Greek History (Open Yale Courses)](https://oyc.yale.edu/classics/clcv-205) - Full 24-lecture university video series by Donald Kagan tracing ancient Greek civilization from the Bronze Age through the fall of Athens.
- [Ancient Greece Declassified Podcast](https://www.greecedeclassified.com/) - Accessible, interview-based scholarly podcast exploring ancient Greek philosophy, political theory, archaeology, and literature.
- [Center for Hellenic Studies (Harvard University)](https://chs.harvard.edu/) - Academic hub providing open-access monographs, scholarly articles, lecture series, and digital publications on Hellenic literature and society.

## Digital Tools & Study Aids

- [Alpheios Reading Tools](https://alpheios.net/) - Free browser extension that provides instant morphological parsing, inflection tables, and dictionary definitions when double-clicking Greek words on any website.
- [Diogenes](https://community.dur.ac.uk/p.j.heslin/Software/Diogenes/) - Free desktop software designed for fast, granular lexical searches across classical databases (TLG, PHI) with integrated LSJ lexica.
- [Greek Font Society (GFS)](https://greekfontsociety-gfs.gr/) - Archive of free, open-source digital typefaces based on historical classical and Renaissance Greek typography.
- [Learning Ancient Greek (The Open University / OpenLearn)](https://fass.open.ac.uk/classical-studies/learning-ancient-greek) - Introductory study guides, taster modules, and independent learning advice for self-directed classicists.
